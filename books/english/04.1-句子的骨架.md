# 04.1 句子的骨架

Mini-English 的基本語序是：**主詞＋動詞＋賓語**。句子通常從誰或什麼開始，再說動作，最後說動作涉及的對象。這個順序可以讓意思清楚，也方便用同一個句型說出更多句子。

## 1. 先找出主詞、動詞和賓語

| 部分 | 作用 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 主詞 | 誰或什麼做動作 | I |
| 動詞 | 做什麼 | eat |
| 賓語 | 對誰或什麼做動作 | rice |

完整句子：**I eat rice.**

可以分成 3 個部分：

| 主詞 | 動詞 | 賓語 |
|---|---|---|
| I | eat | rice |

更多例子：

- **I eat rice.**
- **I read a book.**
- **She drinks milk.**
- **We watch a movie.**
- **They clean the room.**

動詞也可以直接和主詞連接，不需要賓語。

| 主詞 | 動詞 | 完整句子 |
|---|---|---|
| I | am ready | **I am ready.** |
| He | is late | **He is late.** |
| They | are here | **They are here.** |

## 2. 問句的基本順序

### be 動詞問句

把 **am、is、are** 放在主詞前面。

| 肯定句 | 問句 |
|---|---|
| You are ready. | **Are you ready?** |
| She is at home. | **Is she at home?** |
| They are students. | **Are they students?** |

### 一般問句

把 **do、does、can、will** 等放在主詞前面。

| 肯定句 | 問句 |
|---|---|
| I live here. | **Do I live here?** |
| She lives there. | **Does she live there?** |
| He can swim. | **Can he swim?** |
| They will come. | **Will they come?** |

### wh- 問句

把 **who、what、where、when、why、how** 放在句首。

| 問句 | 問的內容 |
|---|---|
| **Who is that?** | 那是誰？ |
| **What is this?** | 這是什麼？ |
| **Where do you live?** | 你住在哪裡？ |
| **When do they arrive?** | 他們什麼時候到？ |
| **Why do you study?** | 你為什麼學習？ |
| **How do you go?** | 你怎麼去？ |

## 3. 加上地點和時間

基本句子仍然是：

**主詞＋動詞＋賓語**

地點和時間可以放在句尾，讓句子更完整。

| 類型 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| 地點 | **I work in a school.** |
| 地點 | **She lives in the city.** |
| 時間 | **They arrive tomorrow.** |
| 地點和時間 | **We meet at the station on Sunday.** |
| 地點和時間 | **I will call you at home tonight.** |

常見的介系詞和位置：

| 位置 | 用法 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 某個地方裡面 | in | **The cat is in the room.** |
| 某個東西上面 | on | **The book is on the table.** |
| 某個地點 | at | **We meet at the school.** |
| 從某個地方 | from | **The train comes from the station.** |
| 和某人一起 | with | **I go home with my friend.** |
| 到某個地方 | to | **She goes to work by bus.** |

## 4. 用 and、or 和 but 連接句子

連接詞可以讓 2 個基本句子變成較長的句子。

### and：和

**I am a student. He is a teacher.**

→ **I am a student and he is a teacher.**

### or：或

**Do you want tea? Do you want coffee?**

→ **Do you want tea or coffee?**

### but：但是

**The room is small. It is clean.**

→ **The room is small but it is clean.**

連接句子時，也可以使用 **because、so、if、when** 和 **while**。

| 連接詞 | 意思 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| because | 因為 | **I stay at home because I am sick.** |
| so | 所以 | **It is late, so I go home.** |
| if | 如果 | **If it rains, I will stay at home.** |
| when | 當……時 | **When I arrive, I call my friend.** |
| while | 當……時 | **I read a book while my mother works.** |

## 練習

- [ ] 把句子改成問句：You are a student.

> 答案：Are you a student?

- [ ] 用正確的順序組成句子：my / reads / father / a / book.

> 答案：My father reads a book.

- [ ] 把句子改成否定句：I have a car.

> 答案：I do not have a car.

- [ ] 用 **and** 連接句子：I am tired. I want to sleep.

> 答案：I am tired and I want to sleep.

- [ ] 用 **where** 提問：They live in a small town.

> 答案：Where do they live?
