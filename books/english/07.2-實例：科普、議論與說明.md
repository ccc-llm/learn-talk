# 07.2 實例：科普、議論與說明

這一章要學習如何用簡單的單字和固定句型，表達科學知識、提出看法，以及說明事情的方法。改寫時，先找出主要的人、事、地點和動作，再用短句連接起来。遇到詞表以外的概念，可以使用「描述 ＋ 類頭」來代替。

## 科普：說明自然與科學

科普文章通常先說出一個事實，再補充原因、過程或結果。Mini-English 可以使用「because」、「when」和「so」連接短句。

| 表達功能 | Mini-English 簡易英文 |
|---|---|
| 說明基本事實 | The sun gives light and heat to the earth. |
| 說明需要什麼 | Plants need water and light. |
| 說明變化 | When the air becomes cold, water becomes ice. |
| 說明自然過程 | The earth goes around the sun. |
| 說明生活環境 | Some animals live in very cold places. |
| 描述詞表以外的概念 | A strong movement of the ground can break buildings. |

### 更多改寫例句

- The moon goes around the earth.
- Trees need water to grow.
- Many birds build their homes in trees.
- Fish can live in water.
- The sun gives light and heat to the earth.
- Plants can make food from light, water, and air.
- When the temperature falls, the water becomes ice.
- A strong movement of the ground can break buildings.

這些句子只使用簡單的主詞、動詞和賓語。科普內容不需要很長，每句只說一個主要意思。

## 議論：提出看法與理由

提出看法時，可以使用「should」、「must」、「can」和「I think」。要說明理由，可以使用「because」。若要表示不同看法，可以使用「but」。

| 表達功能 | Mini-English 簡易英文 |
|---|---|
| 個人看法 | I think people should protect clean air. |
| 建議 | We should use a bus or a bike. |
| 必須做的事 | People must protect forests. |
| 理由 | Clean air is important for our health. |
| 轉折 | I agree, but the price is too high. |
| 可能的效果 | More trees can make cities cooler. |

### 更多改寫例句

- I think people should use a bus or a bike.
- People must protect forests because animals live there.
- Clean air is important for our health.
- More trees can make cities cooler and cleaner.
- I agree, but I do not have enough money for a new car.
- Some people think that electric cars can reduce air pollution.
- We should use less water because clean water is important.
- The weather is nice today, but I must stay at home and work.

## 說明：描述方法與先後順序

說明一件事的方法時，可以使用「first」、「then」、「after that」和「finally」。也可以使用「to + 動詞」表示目的。

| 表達功能 | Mini-English 簡易英文 |
|---|---|
| 先做某事 | First, add some water to the cup. |
| 接著做某事 | Then, put 2 tea bags in the cup. |
| 方法的目的 | Use a clock to check the time. |
| 條件 | If it rains, we will stay at home. |
| 最後一步 | Finally, drink the tea. |

### 更多改寫例句

- First, wash the apple.
- Then, cut the apple into small pieces.
- Finally, put the pieces in a bowl.
- Use a phone to call your friend.
- Use a map to find the right way.
- If the light is on, close the door and turn off the light.
- After you come home, put your bag on the table.
- When you enter the room, turn on the light.

### 過程改寫對照

| 說明順序 | Mini-English 簡易英文 |
|---|---|
| 步驟 1 | First, wash your hands. |
| 步驟 2 | Then, take the cup. |
| 步驟 3 | Next, pour the water into the cup. |
| 步驟 4 | Finally, drink the water. |

## 練習

- [ ] 完成句子：The sun gives ___ and ___ to the earth.

> 答案：light and heat

- [ ] 將下列 2 個句子合成 1 句：Plants need water. Plants need light.

> 答案：Plants need water and light.

- [ ] 將「我們應該保護環境，因為環境影響健康。」改寫成 Mini-English。

> 答案：We should protect the environment because it affects health.

- [ ] 將下列句子改成使用「if」的句子：I will stay at home. It will rain.

> 答案：If it rains, I will stay at home.

- [ ] 依照句子「The train leaves at 7 o'clock.」寫一個「when」問句。

> 答案：When does the train leave?
