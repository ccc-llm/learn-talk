# 02.3 數量、時間、方位・地點

本章學習描述「多少、什麼時候、在哪裡」。你可以使用數量詞表達大小與數量，使用時間詞表達日期、月份和一天中的時間，也使用方位詞與介系詞表達位置。回答問題時，先想清楚 **how many / how much**、**when**、**where** 這 3 種問題，再選擇適合的句子。

## 數量：有多少

| Mini-English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| many | 許多；很多 |
| much | 許多；很多 |
| a few | 少數；一些 |
| few | 很少 |
| a little | 一點；少量 |
| some | 一些 |
| all | 全部 |
| every | 每一個 |
| half | 一半 |
| first | 第一 |
| next | 下一個 |
| number | 數字；號碼 |
| double | 兩倍 |
| twice | 兩次 |

`many` 和 `much` 都可以表示「許多」，但是通常用 `many + 可數名詞複數`，用 `much + 不可數名詞`：

- I have many books.
- There is much water in the cup.

`a few` 和 `few` 都可以和可數名詞複數一起使用。`a few` 表示「有一些」，`few` 表示「很少」：

- I have a few friends.
- I have few friends today.

`a little` 和 `little` 可以和不可數名詞一起使用。`a little` 表示「有一點」，`little` 表示「很少」：

- There is a little milk in the glass.
- There is little water in the bottle.

`all` 表示「全部」，`every` 表示「每一個」。`every` 後面接單數名詞：

- I want all the food.
- Every student in my class has a phone.

`half` 表示「一半」，`double` 表示「兩倍」，`twice` 表示「兩次」：

- Half of the work is done.
- I need double the time.
- I go to the market twice a week.

## 時間：什麼時候

| Mini-English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| today | 今天 |
| tomorrow | 明天 |
| yesterday | 昨天 |
| now | 現在 |
| then | 那時；然後 |
| soon | 很快 |
| already | 已經 |
| yet | 還；尚未 |
| morning | 早上 |
| afternoon | 下午 |
| evening | 傍晚 |
| night | 夜晚 |
| today night | 今天晚上 |
| today morning | 今天早上 |
| week | 星期；週 |
| month | 月 |
| year | 年 |
| Monday | 星期一 |
| Friday | 星期五 |
| Sunday | 星期日 |
| weekend | 週末 |
| holiday | 假日 |
| birthday | 生日 |
| early | 早 |
| late | 晚；遲 |
| o'clock | 點鐘 |

時間可以放在句首、句尾或動詞前面：

- I work at the school today.
- We will go to the park tomorrow.
- She was very tired yesterday.

時間和動詞一起使用時，常用 `at`、`on` 或 `in`：

- I get up at 6 o'clock every morning.
- We have a class on Monday.
- They travel in July.

`today`、`tomorrow` 和 `yesterday` 前面通常不加 `in`、`on` 或 `at`。月份和年份通常使用 `in`：

- I was born in 1990.
- We visit our family in August.

`a week`、`a month` 和 `a year` 可以表示一段時間：

- I study English every day for 2 hours.
- We stay at home for a week.
- She has worked here for a year.

### 在地點

`in` 表示在裡面，`on` 表示在表面上，`at` 表示在某個地點：

- The book is in the bag.
- The cup is on the table.
- We meet at the bus stop.

其他常見的位置說法如下：

- The cat is under the chair.
- The clock is above the door.
- The car is behind the house.
- The shop is between the bank and the school.
- My home is near the park.
- The teacher stands beside the door.
- The bus stop is in front of the station.
- The children play around the tree.
- We cross the road together.
- He walks through the door.
- We walk along the street.
- The car moves toward the city.
- The train comes from the station.
- We go to the museum.
- The keys are inside the car.
- The dog is outside the house.

### 詢問位置

`where` 可以用來詢問「在哪裡」：

- Where is the school?
- Where are my keys?
- Where do you live?

回答地點問題時，可以使用 `at`、`in` 或 `on`：

- The school is on this street.
- My keys are in the bag.
- I live in a small city.

## 對話

甲：Where is the bus stop?  
乙：It is in front of the bank.  
甲：When do you go there?  
乙：I go there at 8 o'clock every morning.  
甲：How many people wait for the bus?  
乙：About 10 people wait there now.

## 數量、時間與方位的組合

數量、時間和方位可以一起出現在同一個句子裡：

- There are 3 people in the room now.
- I will buy 2 apples at the market tomorrow.
- We have many books on the table.
- The train leaves the station at 5 o'clock.
- There is a long line in front of the door.
- I read 2 books every week.
- She has a new job in another city.
- We stay at home for the whole weekend.
- The children play in the park every afternoon.
- The shop is at the corner of the street.
