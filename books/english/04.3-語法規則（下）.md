# 04.3 語法規則（下）

本章學習編號語法規則的後半部分。內容包括所有格、複數、形容詞與副詞、介系詞、連接詞、比較、能力與義務、數量、目的和存在句型。每個規則都用 Mini-English 簡易英文說明，讓你學會把單字組合成更多句子。

## 所有格、複數與形容詞

### 所有格

規則 11：表示「某人的」，可以在名詞後面加 `'s`。

| 說明 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| 我的書 | This is my book. |
| 姐姐的書 | This is my sister's book. |
| 老師的教室 | This is the teacher's classroom. |

### 複數

規則 12：一般名詞加 `-s` 或 `-es`；有些名詞有固定的不規則複數。

| 單數 | 複數 | 說明 |
|---|---|---|
| book | books | 一般加 `-s` |
| box | boxes | 以 `-x` 結尾，加 `-es` |
| baby | babies | 以輔音字母加 `-y` 結尾，改為 `-ies` |
| child | children | 不規則複數 |
| foot | feet | 不規則複數 |
| woman | women | 不規則複數 |

**完整例句：**

- I have many books and 3 pens.
- These children play in the park.
- The teacher has a new computer.

### 形容詞與副詞

規則 13：形容詞放在名詞前面。副詞可以放在動詞前後，也可以放在形容詞前面。

| 用法 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| 形容詞 + 名詞 | a beautiful day |
| very + 形容詞 | a very good day |
| 副詞 | She runs fast. |

**完整例句：**

- She has a very long hair.
- He runs very fast.
- The weather is cold today.

## 介系詞與連接詞

### 介系詞

規則 14：介系詞放在名詞或代詞前面，表示地點、方向、方式或關係。

| 介系詞 | 意思 | Mini-English |
|---|---|---|
| in | 在裡面 | The book is in the bag. |
| on | 在上面 | The book is on the table. |
| at | 在地點或時間 | I am at home now. |
| to | 到；為了 | I go to school. |
| for | 為了；給 | This gift is for you. |
| with | 和……一起 | I go home with a friend. |
| from | 從 | I come from China. |
| by | 靠……；搭乘 | I go to work by bus. |

**完整例句：**

- I go to work with a friend.
- The book is on the table.
- We wait for the bus at the bus stop.

### 連接詞

規則 15：使用 `and`、`or`、`but`、`because`、`so`、`if`、`when`、`until`、`while` 等連接詞，把 2 個句子或短語連在一起。

| 連接詞 | 意思 | Mini-English |
|---|---|---|
| and | 和 | I have a pen and a book. |
| or | 或 | Tea or coffee? |
| but | 但是 | I want to go, but I am busy. |
| because | 因為 | I go home because I am tired. |
| so | 所以 | It is late, so I go home. |
| if | 如果 | If it rains, I will stay at home. |
| when | 當……時 | I call you when I arrive. |
| until | 直到 | Wait here until I come back. |
| while | 當……時 | She reads a book while I work. |

**完整例句：**

- I go home because I am tired.
- If it rains, I will stay at home.
- You must wait here until I come back.

## 比較、能力與義務

### 比較

規則 16：比較 2 個事物時，可以使用 `more ... than` 或 `as ... as`。最高級通常使用 `-est`。

| 比較方式 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| more ... than | He is more tall than me. |
| as ... as | She runs as fast as her brother. |
| 最高級 | This is the best book. |

**完整例句：**

- He is taller than me.
- She runs as fast as her brother.
- This is the most important day of the year.

### 能力與義務

規則 17：`can` 表示能力，`must` 表示必須，`should` 表示應該。

| 形式 | 意思 | Mini-English |
|---|---|---|
| can + V | 能夠 | I can swim. |
| must + V | 必須 | You must wait here. |
| should + V | 應該 | You should go now. |

**完整例句：**

- I can swim very well.
- You must wait here until I come back.
- You should see a doctor.

## 數量、目的與存在

### 數量

規則 18：使用 `many`、`much`、`a few`、`few`、`some`、`all`、`every`、`half` 等詞表示數量。

| 詞 | 意思 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| many | 許多，可數 | many books |
| much | 許多，不可數 | much water |
| a few | 一些，可數 | a few friends |
| few | 很少，可數 | few people |
| some | 一些 | some food |
| all | 全部 | all students |
| every | 每一個 | every student |
| half | 一半 | half of the work |

**完整例句：**

- I have many books and a little water.
- Every student in my class has a phone.
- Half of the work is already finished.

### 目的

規則 19：使用 `to + V` 表示目的，也就是「為了做某事」。

| 說明 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| 為了買食物 | I go to the market to buy food. |
| 為了說 hello | She calls me to say hello. |
| 為了學英文 | I study English to speak with people. |

**完整例句：**

- I go to the market to buy food.
- She calls me to say hello.
- I come here to learn English.

### 存在

規則 20：使用 `there is` 或 `there are` 表示某個地方有某物。

| 形式 | 意思 | Mini-English |
|---|---|---|
| there is + 單數或不可數名詞 | 有某物 | There is a book on the table. |
| there are + 複數名詞 | 有某些東西 | There are many people in the park. |

**完整例句：**

- There is a book on the table.
- There are many people in the park.
- There is no water in the cup.

## 對話

甲：What is this thing for?

乙：It is a tool for cutting.

甲：There are many books on the table.

乙：Can you give me one book?

甲：Yes. I can give you this book.

甲：Are you ready?

乙：Yes. I am ready.
