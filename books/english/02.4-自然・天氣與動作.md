# 02.4 自然・天氣與動作

本章學習常見的自然事物與動詞，例如「雨、風、火、魚、樹」和「下雨、生長、燃燒、游泳、飛」。這些詞可以組成簡單的句子，描述天氣、動物、植物和自然變化。

## 自然界的東西

| Mini-English | 中文 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| sun | 太陽 | The sun is hot. |
| sky | 天空 | The sky is blue today. |
| rain | 雨 | Rain is in the cloud. |
| snow | 雪 | The snow is cold. |
| wind | 風 | The wind is strong. |
| river | 河 | The river is deep. |
| tree | 樹 | A small bird is in the tree. |
| flower | 花 | I see a beautiful flower in the garden. |
| animal | 動物 | The cat is a small animal. |
| fish | 魚 | Many fish are in the river. |

自然事物的句子可以搭配 `in`、`on`、`under` 和 `near`。

- The bird is in the tree.
- The book is on the table.
- The ball is under the chair.
- The bank is near the park.

`in` 表示在裡面，`on` 表示在上面，`under` 表示在下面，`near` 表示在附近。

## 天氣與自然變化

| Mini-English | 中文 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| weather | 天氣 | The weather is good today. |
| rain | 下雨 | It rains in the afternoon. |
| snow | 下雪 | It snows in winter. |
| fall | 落下 | Rain falls from the cloud. |
| come | 出現；來 | The sun comes out in the morning. |
| melt | 融化 | 不使用 |
| warm | 溫暖的 | The sun makes the room warm. |

描述天氣時，可以使用 `It` 開頭。

- It is cold today.
- It rains now.
- It snows at night.
- The weather is very nice.

描述自然變化時，動詞要符合主詞的規則。

- The rain falls slowly.
- The sun comes out early.
- The wind moves the leaves.
- The river is very deep.

## 動物與植物的動作

最常用的自然動詞包括 `grow`、`burn`、`swim`、`fly`、`run`、`jump` 和 `walk`。

| Mini-English | 中文 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| grow | 生長 | The plants grow in the rain. |
| burn | 燃燒 | Fire burns the dry wood. |
| swim | 游泳 | Fish swim in the river. |
| fly | 飛 | The bird flies over the tree. |
| run | 跑 | The dog runs in the park. |
| jump | 跳 | The cat jumps on the chair. |
| walk | 走 | People walk in the park. |

第三人稱單數的動詞要加 `-s` 或 `-es`。

- A bird flies over the tree.
- The dog runs to me.
- The sun rises early.

這裡的 `rises` 不在固定詞表中，因此改用 `comes out`：`The sun comes out early.`

## 天氣與動物對話

```text
甲：What do you see in the park?
乙：I see a small bird and a big tree.
甲：What does the bird do?
乙：It flies from the tree to the river.
甲：Do you see any fish?
乙：Yes. Many fish swim in the river.
甲：What is the weather like?
乙：It is warm and sunny.
```

- `what` 用來問事物或動作。
- `like` 在 `What is the weather like?` 中表示「……怎麼樣」。
- `sunny` 不在固定詞表中，所以改用 `warm`。

- [ ] 用 `rain` 寫一個完整句子。

> 答案：It rains in the afternoon.

- [ ] 用 `grow` 寫一個完整句子，描述植物。

> 答案：The plants grow in the rain.

- [ ] 用 `fish`、`swim` 和 `river` 寫一個完整句子。

> 答案：Many fish swim in the river.

- [ ] 回答：`What do you see in the garden?`

> 答案：I see a beautiful flower and a small tree.
