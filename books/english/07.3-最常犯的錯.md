# 07.3 最常犯的錯

學習者最常犯的錯，通常是因為相同的規則沒有分清楚。這一章學習 be 動詞、冠詞、複數、第三人稱單數和常用介系詞的正確用法，並把常見的錯誤句改成自然英文。

## 1. be 動詞不要漏掉

在英文中，句子需要一個 be 動詞。I 後面用 am；you、we、they 後面用 are；he、she、it 後面用 is。

| 錯誤 | 正確 |
|---|---|
| I student. | I am a student. |
| You ready? | Are you ready? |
| She at home. | She is at home. |

**問句**要把 be 動詞放在句首。

- Are you ready?
- Is she at home?
- Are they here?

否定句在 be 動詞後面加 not。

- I am not tired.
- She is not at home.
- They are not ready.

## 2. 冠詞和複數要一致

第一次提到一個不特定的人或物時，使用 a 或 an。再次提到同一個人或物時，使用 the。名詞表示多個時，通常要加 -s 或 -es。

| 錯誤 | 正確 |
|---|---|
| This is a apple. | This is an apple. |
| I have a book. The book is here. | I have the book. The book is here. |
| I have 2 book. | I have 2 books. |
| There are many bus. | There are many buses. |

**不可數名詞**通常不能加 -s，也不用 a 或 an。這個規則可以用來說明食物和材料。

- I drink water.
- She eats rice.
- We need some water.

如果要說「許多」，可以使用 many、several 或 some。

- I have many books.
- She has 3 bags.
- There are some people in the park.

## 3. 第三人稱單數要加 -s

主詞是 he、she 或 it 時，一般現在式的動詞要加 -s。動詞以 -o、-s、-x、-ch 或 -sh 結尾時，要加 -es。

| 錯誤 | 正確 |
|---|---|
| He go to work by bus. | He goes to work by bus. |
| She watch TV every night. | She watches TV every night. |
| It rain today. | It rains today. |

否定句要使用 does not。

- He does not go to work by bus.
- She does not watch TV every night.
- It does not rain today.

一般疑問句要把 does 放在句首。

- Does he go to work by bus?
- Does she watch TV every night?
- Does it rain today?

## 4. 星期和時間要用正確的介系詞

星期、日期和月份前面通常使用 on；鐘點前面使用 at。

| 錯誤 | 正確 |
|---|---|
| I work in Monday. | I work on Monday. |
| We meet at Friday. | We meet on Friday. |
| The shop opens in 8 o'clock. | The shop opens at 8 o'clock. |

時間前可以使用 on、at、in。

- I work on Monday.
- We meet on Friday.
- The class starts at 9 o'clock.
- I was at home in the evening.

**對話**

甲：Are you ready?  
乙：Yes, I am. I am ready now.  
甲：Does she work on Monday?  
乙：Yes, she works on Monday.  
甲：Are there any buses at the bus stop?  
乙：Yes, there are 2 buses.

## 練習

- [ ] 我是學生。  
   
> 答案：I am a student.

- [ ] 她有 2 個哥哥。  
   
> 答案：She has 2 brothers.

- [ ] 他乘公車上班。  
   
> 答案：He goes to work by bus.

- [ ] 我們星期一見面。  
   
> 答案：We meet on Monday.

- [ ] 商店 8 點開門。  
   
> 答案：The shop opens at 8 o'clock.
