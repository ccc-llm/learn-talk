# 04.2 語法規則（上）

本章學習前 10 個基本語法規則，包括句子結構、be 動詞、第三人稱單數、進行式、過去式、將來式、否定句、問句與冠詞。這些規則可以幫助你用 Mini-English 組合更多句子。

## 1. 基本句型與 be 動詞

### 規則 1：主詞＋動詞＋賓語

大部分簡單句都使用這個結構。

| 句子部分 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| 主詞 | I |
| 動詞 | eat |
| 賓語 | rice |

- I eat rice every day.
- She writes a letter to her friend.
- We study English at school.

### 規則 2：使用正確的 be 動詞

| 主詞 | be 動詞 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am a student. |
| he / she / it | is | She is my teacher. |
| you / we / they | are | You are my friend. |
| 過去式，單數 | was | He was very tired yesterday. |
| 過去式，複數 | were | They were at home. |

be 動詞也可以用於問題：

- Are you ready?
- Is this your phone?

### 規則 3：第三人稱單數

主詞是 he、she 或 it 時，一般現在式的動詞要加 -s 或 -es。

| 原形 | 第三人稱單數 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| go | goes | He goes to work by bus. |
| work | works | My mother works in a shop. |
| watch | watches | She watches the game. |
| have | has | He has a car. |

### 規則 4：進行式

進行式表示動作現在正在發生。結構是：

> be 動詞 + 動詞加 -ing

| 句子 | 說明 |
|---|---|
| I am eating lunch now. | 我現在正在吃午餐。 |
| They are waiting for the bus. | 他們正在等公車。 |
| She is reading a book. | 她正在讀一本書。 |

## 2. 時態與否定句

### 規則 5：過去式

規則動詞直接加 -ed；不規則動詞使用指定形式。

| 原形 | 過去式 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| go | went | I went to the doctor yesterday. |
| cook | cooked | She cooked dinner for her family. |
| buy | bought | They bought some food. |
| see | saw | I saw him at the station. |
| write | wrote | He wrote a letter to me. |

否定句在 be 動詞後面加 not；在一般動詞前面加 do not 或 does not。

- I did not go home yesterday.
- She did not cook dinner.
- He does not work on Sunday.

### 規則 6：將來式

將來式使用 will + 原形動詞。

| 結構 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|
| I will + 動詞 | I will call you tomorrow. |
| He will + 動詞 | He will go to work by bus. |
| We will + 動詞 | We will go to the market on Sunday. |

也可以在 will 後使用 not：

- I will not be late.
- She will not come today.

### 規則 7：否定句

否定句有兩種常見形式。

| 形式 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| be 動詞 + not | I am not tired. |
| do / does + not + 原形動詞 | I do not like this one. |
| there is + no | There is no water in the cup. |
| there are + no | There are no people in the room. |

更多例子：

- The book is not on the table.
- They do not work at home today.
- She does not have a new phone.

## 3. 問句

### 規則 8：是非問句

把 be 動詞、助動詞或 do 移到句首。

| 類型 | 問句 | 完整例子 |
|---|---|---|
| be 動詞 | Are you ready? | Yes, I am. |
| be 動詞 | Is she at home? | No, she is not. |
| 助動詞 | Can she sing? | Yes, she can. |
| 助動詞 | Do you work here? | Yes, I do. |
| do / does | Does he like it? | Yes, he does. |

### 規則 9：wh- 問句

使用 who、what、where、when、why、how 或 which 提問。

| 問題 | 意思 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| who | 誰 | Who is that? |
| what | 什麼 | What is this thing for? |
| where | 哪裡 | Where do you work? |
| when | 何時 | When will you come? |
| why | 為什麼 | Why are you here? |
| how | 怎麼 | How much is it? |
| which | 哪一個 | Which one do you want? |

完整問答例子：

- Where is my phone? — It is on the table.
- When will you come? — I will come tomorrow.
- Who is your teacher? — This is my teacher.

### 對話

甲：Do you work here?  
乙：Yes, I work in this shop.  
甲：Where is the shop?  
乙：It is on this street.  
甲：Do you have a map?  
乙：Yes, here it is.

## 4. 冠詞 a、an 與 the

### 規則 10：選擇正確的冠詞

| 冠詞 | 使用方式 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| a | 第一次提到一個不特定的單數名詞 | This is a small shop. |
| an | 用於元音發音前的單數名詞 | I want an apple. |
| the | 再次提到，或特別指定某個事物 | The shop is on this street. |

### a 與 an

| 名詞 | 冠詞 | 完整例句 |
|---|---|---|
| apple | an | I want an apple. |
| egg | an | She has an egg. |
| book | a | This is a good book. |
| car | a | He has a car. |

### 再次提到時使用 the

- I see a shop. The shop is very small.
- She has a book. The book is new.
- They live in a city. The city is very big.

### 特別指定時使用 the

- The door is open.
- The bus stop is near my home.
- The people in the room are my friends.

表達「有某個事物」時，使用 there is 或 there are：

| 單數 | 複數 |
|---|---|
| There is a book on the table. | There are many books on the table. |

- There is a problem.
- There is no water in the cup.
- There are 2 chairs near the window.

## 練習

- [ ] 用 Mini-English 說出：「我現在正在吃午餐。」

> 答案：I am eating lunch now.

- [ ] 用 Mini-English 問：「你在這裡工作嗎？」

> 答案：Do you work here?

- [ ] 用 Mini-English 說出：「她昨天很累。」

> 答案：She was very tired yesterday.

- [ ] 用 Mini-English 說出：「我想要一個蘋果。」

> 答案：I want an apple.

- [ ] 將「桌子上有一本書。」改寫成否定句。

> 答案：There is no book on the table.
