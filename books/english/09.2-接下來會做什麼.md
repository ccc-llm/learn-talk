## 三、想做什麼：want to / hope to / plan to

## 四、把計畫說清楚：時間詞、順序和對話

時間詞表：

| 中文 | Mini-English | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 明天 | tomorrow | I will call you tomorrow. |
| 今晚 | tonight | We will cook dinner tonight. |
| 下週 | next week | She will start a new job next week. |
| 下個月 | next month | He is going to buy a car next month. |
| 明年 | next year | I want to learn English next year. |
| 這個週末 | this weekend | I am going to visit my family this weekend. |
| 很快 | soon | I hope to see you soon. |

排順序：first（第一）、then（然後）、after that（在那之後）、before（在…之前）。

例句：
1. First, I will do my work. Then, I will go home.
2. After that, I will rest.
3. Tomorrow morning, I am going to study English. After that, I am going to meet a friend.
4. Before I go to bed, I will read a book.
5. I will call you after I finish my work.

對話：

甲：What are you going to do this weekend?
乙：I am going to visit my parents. We will cook dinner together.
甲：That is nice! I want to see a movie.
乙：Do you want to come with us on Sunday?
甲：Yes! I will call you on Saturday.

句型總整理：

| 句型 | 意思 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| will + 動詞 | 將來會做 | I will call you tomorrow. |
| am / is / are going to + 動詞 | 打算要做 | I am going to visit my family. |
| want / hope / plan to + 動詞 | 想做、希望做 | I hope to see you soon. |
| First, ... Then, ... / After that, ... | 排順序 | First, I will do my work. Then, I will go home. |
