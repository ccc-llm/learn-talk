# 04.5 自然度：讀起來會不會怪

本章要學習怎麼讓句子讀起來自然。自然的意思是：句子中的字、動詞形式、介系詞和連接詞，都按照母語者常用的方式放在一起。這個章節會說明詞序、常見搭配、問句和常用句型，幫助你寫出正確又自然的句子。

## 1. 自然的詞序

英文句子常有固定順序。主詞放在最前面，動詞放在中間，賓語放在後面。時間、方式和地方通常放在句尾。

| 不自然 | 自然 | 為什麼 |
|---|---|---|
| My mother in a shop works. | My mother works in a shop. | 主詞後面先放動詞，再放地方。 |
| Every day I go by bus to work. | I go to work by bus every day. | 時間放在句尾很自然。 |
| A cup of tea on the table is. | There is a cup of tea on the table. | 描述位置時使用 `There is` 或 `There are`。 |
| I very like this book. | I like this book very much. | `like` 後面放受詞；`very much` 放在句尾。 |

這些句子使用同一個意思：

- I go to work by bus every day.
- My mother works in a shop.
- There is a cup of tea on the table.
- I like this book very much.

`There is` 和 `There are` 是很常用的說法。`is` 後面接單數或不可數的名詞，`are` 後面接複數的名詞。

- There is a book on the table.
- There are many books on the table.
- There is a cup of tea on the table.
- There are 3 people in the room.

## 2. 自然的名詞和動詞搭配

有些動詞和名詞經常一起使用。這些組合不需要背很多規則，只要多看例子，就能慢慢記住。

| 動詞和名詞 | 自然的例句 | 中文意思 |
|---|---|---|
| `have` ＋ `breakfast` | I have breakfast at home. | 我在家裡吃早餐。 |
| `take` ＋ `bus` | I take the bus to work. | 我搭公車去上班。 |
| `watch` ＋ `film` | We watch a film on Sunday. | 我們星期日看電影。 |
| `pay` ＋ `bill` | I pay the bill now. | 我現在付帳。 |
| `wash` ＋ `hands` | He washes his hands before dinner. | 他在吃飯前洗手。 |
| `make` ＋ `dinner` | My father makes dinner. | 我爸爸做晚餐。 |

有些動詞也常用固定的受詞：

- Wash your face.
- Wash your hands.
- Close the door.
- Open the window.
- Clean the room.
- Make the bed.
- Take a bus.
- Take a photo.
- Pay the bill.
- Do your work.

不要為了變化而把常見的搭配換成不常用的說法。下面第一句比較自然，第二句雖然可以猜出意思，但讀起來會很怪：

- She takes care of her dog.
- She takes her dog of care.

`take care of` 的意思是「照顧」。因為 `care` 是表內的詞，所以可以直接用這個固定搭配。

其他自然的固定搭配還有：

- pay attention
- take care
- have breakfast
- have lunch
- have dinner
- come back
- go home
- get up
- sit down
- look at
- listen to
- talk to
- wait for

## 3. 自然的問句和回答

問句要把 `be` 動詞、`can` 或 `do` 移到主詞前面。

### `be` 動詞問句

- Are you ready?
- Is she your teacher?
- Is this your bag?
- Were they at home?

回答時可以用 `yes` 加上肯定的句子，或用 `not` 加上否定的句子：

- Yes, I am ready.
- No, I am not ready.
- Yes, she is my teacher.
- No, this is not my bag.

### `do` 動詞問句

- Do you live here?
- Does he work at night?
- Do they speak English?

回答時要把 `do` 或 `does` 放回句中：

- Yes, I do.
- No, I do not live here.
- Yes, he does.
- No, he does not work at night.

### `can` 問句

- Can she sing?
- Can you help me?
- Can they swim?

回答時可以保持簡短：

- Yes, she can.
- No, she cannot.
- Yes, I can.
- No, I cannot help you.

問句之後常用 `or` 讓對方選擇：

- Do you want tea or coffee?
- Is this one or that one?
- Do you come on Monday or Tuesday?

## 4. 自然的語氣和常用短句

自然的句子不一定很長。很多時候，一個短句更自然，也更容易說清楚。

### 回答問題

- Yes, I do.
- No, I do not know.
- Maybe.
- I am not sure.
- That is correct.
- Thank you.
- You are welcome.

### 說明原因

- I go home because I am tired.
- She stays at home because it rains.
- I like this book because it is very interesting.
- We go to the park because the weather is good.

### 說明接下來要做的事

- I will call you tomorrow.
- She will arrive at 7.
- We will go to the market on Sunday.
- They will work together.

### 說明兩件事同時進行

- I am eating lunch now.
- She is waiting for the bus.
- They are working at the office.
- We are reading a book together.

### 描述一個人

- He is a good teacher.
- She is my older sister.
- My father is a hard worker.
- They are very kind people.

自然度不只取決於句子是否正確，也取決於字詞是否常一起出現、句子的長度是否適合意思，以及問句和回答是否互相配合。多讀下面的句子，再把相同的結構換成自己的字，就能逐步寫出更自然的句子。

## 練習

- [ ] 把下面不自然的句子改成自然的句子：My friend in the shop works.

> 答案：My friend works in the shop.

- [ ] 用 `There is` 完成句子：___ a cup of tea on the table.

> 答案：There is a cup of tea on the table.

- [ ] 把問句和回答配對起來。問句：`Do you speak English?` 回答：`Yes, I do.`

> 答案：Do you speak English? — Yes, I do.

- [ ] 用 `because` 寫一個完整的句子，說明你為什麼要回家。

> 答案：I go home because I am tired.

- [ ] 把 `make` 換成另一個動詞，改寫句子：`My mother makes dinner.` 答案必須使用表內的詞。

> 答案：My mother cooks dinner.
