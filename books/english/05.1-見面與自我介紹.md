# 05.1 見面與自我介紹

本章要學習見面時的問候、自我介紹，以及說出自己的名字、所在城市、語言和職業。這些句型也可以用來介紹朋友或認識新的人。

## 一、問候與見面

第一次見面時，可以用簡單的問候開始，再說「很高興認識你」。

| 中文 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| 你好 | Hello. |
| 嗨 | Hi. |
| 你好嗎？ | How are you? |
| 我很好，謝謝。 | I am very well, thank you. |
| 很高興認識你。 | Nice to meet you. |
| 歡迎來我家。 | Welcome to my home. |
| 請進。 | Please come in. |
| 請坐。 | Please sit down. |

**完整例句：**

- Hello, I am a teacher.
- Hi, I am a student.
- How are you?
- I am very well, thank you.
- Nice to meet you.
- Welcome to my home.
- Please sit down.

### 見面對話

```text
甲：Hello. How are you?
乙：I am very well, thank you. Nice to meet you.
甲：My name is Peter (a name of a person).
乙：Nice to meet you too. I am a student.
```

## 二、自我介紹

自我介紹時，可以依序說出名字、职业、所在城市和會說的語言。

| 中文 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| 我的名字是…… | My name is … |
| 我是一名老師。 | I am a teacher. |
| 我是一名學生。 | I am a student. |
| 我住在…… | I live in … |
| 我在學校工作。 | I work at a school. |
| 我會說中文和英文。 | I speak Chinese and English. |
| 我是中國人。 | I am Chinese. |

**完整例句：**

- My name is Peter (a name of a person).
- I am a teacher.
- I am a student.
- I live in Taipei (a city).
- I work at a school.
- I speak Chinese and English.
- I am Chinese.

### 自我介紹對話

```text
甲：Hello. My name is Peter (a name of a person). I am a teacher.
乙：Hi, I am Anna (a name of a person). I am a student.
甲：Where are you from?
乙：I am from Taipei (a city). I live in Taipei.
甲：What do you do?
乙：I work at a school. I teach English.
```

## 三、介紹朋友與其他人

介紹別人時，可以用「This is …」開頭，再用「He is …」或「She is …」說明那個人的事情。

| 中文 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| 這是…… | This is … |
| 他是…… | He is … |
| 她是…… | She is … |
| 他是我的朋友。 | He is my friend. |
| 她是我的同事。 | She is my coworker. |
| 他是醫生。 | He is a doctor. |
| 她是護士。 | She is a nurse. |
| 他是中國人。 | He is Chinese. |

**完整例句：**

- This is my friend.
- He is a doctor.
- She is a nurse.
- He is my father.
- She is my sister.
- He is a good teacher.
- She is a young woman.

### 介紹對話

```text
甲：Who is that?
乙：That is my father. He is a teacher.
甲：Who is she?
乙：She is my mother. She is a nurse.
甲：Nice to meet you.
乙：Nice to meet you too.
```

## 四、常用句型組合

把問候、自我介紹和問候再說一次組合起來，就是一段完整的見面對話。

| 功能 | Mini-English |
|---|---|
| 開始問候 | Hello. / Hi. |
| 詢問對方狀況 | How are you? |
| 回答狀況 | I am very well, thank you. |
| 說出自己的名字 | My name is … |
| 說出自己的職業 | I am a … / I work at a … |
| 說所在城市 | I am from … / I live in … |
| 說會說的語言 | I speak … |
| 介紹別人 | This is … |
| 說很高興認識對方 | Nice to meet you. |

**完整例句：**

- Hello, I am Anna (a name of a person). I am a nurse.
- I am from Taipei (a city) and I live in Taipei.
- I speak Chinese, and I also speak English.
- This is my friend. He is a student.
- Nice to meet you, and welcome to my home.
- I work at a school, and I teach English.

### 完整見面對話

```text
甲：Hello. How are you?
乙：I am very well, thank you. My name is Anna (a name of a person).
甲：Nice to meet you. I am Peter (a name of a person).
乙：I live in Taipei (a city). I am a nurse.
甲：This is my friend. He is a teacher.
乙：Nice to meet you. Welcome to my home.
甲：Thank you. Please come in.
```
