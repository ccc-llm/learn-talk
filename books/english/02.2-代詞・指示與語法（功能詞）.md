Articles:

| 冠詞 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| a | 子音開頭的單數名詞 | I have a cat and a dog. |
| an | 母音開頭的單數名詞 | I eat an apple every morning. |
| the | 對方知道是哪一個 | The book is on the table. |

Prepositions table:

| 介系詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| in | 在…裡 | The key is in the box. |
| on | 在…上 | My phone is on the table. |
| at | 在（地點） | He is at the door. |
| to | 到、往 | I go to school. |
| from | 從 | She comes from a small town. |
| with | 和…一起 | I go to school with my friend. |

Conjunctions:

| 連接詞 | 意思 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| and | 和 | I have a cat and a dog. |
| or | 或 | Do you want tea or coffee? |
| but | 但是 | He is tall but she is short. |
| because | 因為 | I stay at home because it rains. |
| so | 所以 | It rains, so I stay at home. |

Then separate examples for 賓格 and 所有格:

賓格（當受詞）：
- Give me the key.
- I see him every day.
- I see her at the shop.

所有格：
- This is my sister's book.（'s 也是所有格：妹妹的書）
- Their house is big.
- Our teacher is very good.
- Your car is new. / His phone is new.

Tip about 外來語 names:
小提醒：遇到名字時，用外來語，第一次出現加括號說明：
- Ken (a person) is my friend. He is a teacher.
